## Executive Summary
This retainership outlines a Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR) engagement
for a mid-market enterprise. The approach covers 24x7 triage, containment guidance,
forensic imaging, malware analysis, and post-incident reporting with tabletop exercises.

## Scope
- Incident triage and severity classification
- Endpoint and server forensic acquisition (disk and memory where authorized)
- Timeline reconstruction and root-cause hypotheses
- Containment and eradication recommendations
- Executive and technical report packs
- Knowledge transfer workshop (one session)

Out of scope: managed detection as a 24x7 SOC replacement, legal testimony unless separately contracted.

## Technical Approach
1. Intake and evidence preservation checklist
2. Parallel host and network artifact collection
3. Correlation against threat intelligence patterns (generic TTPs)
4. Containment playbooks for ransomware, BEC, and insider scenarios
5. Lessons-learned and detection-gap register

## Methodology
Engagement follows an IR lifecycle: prepare → detect/analyze → contain → eradicate → recover → lessons learned.
Retainership hours are drawn against severity bands ({{RATE_BAND_A}} / {{RATE_BAND_B}}) without binding client-specific commercials.

## Timeline
- Kickoff and access: week 1
- Baseline IR readiness review: weeks 1-2
- Retainer active: rolling 12 months
- Quarterly tabletop: scheduled mutually

## Assumptions
- Named technical contacts available within agreed SLA
- Forensic imaging authorized under client change control
- Cloud log retention sufficient for 90-day lookback where applicable
